FAQ

Questions before you get started

How long does SEO take for an interior design business?
Local SEO changes can show movement within a few weeks. Ranking for style- and project-specific searches typically takes longer, depending on your market.
Is Instagram enough, or do I need a website too?
Both work better together. Instagram builds recognition and shows range; a website gives you a place that ranks in Google search and holds your full portfolio in one organized place.
Do you help with photographing or presenting my projects?
We don't do photography ourselves, but we'll tell you honestly if your current photos are holding your marketing back, and how to prioritize fixing that.
Can an AI chatbot actually qualify design inquiries?
It can capture initial interest, answer common process and pricing questions, and flag serious leads for you to follow up personally.
What should an interior designer budget for digital marketing?
Most designers can only take on a handful of active projects at once, so the goal usually isn't maximum lead volume. It's a steady trickle of the right kind of inquiry that matches how much you can actually take on.
