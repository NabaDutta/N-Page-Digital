FAQ

Questions before you get started

Can a small store actually compete with big marketplaces in search?
Not on broad category terms, realistically — but on specific product searches, local delivery advantages, and trust signals, yes. That's exactly where we focus.
How long does ecommerce SEO take to show results?
Product-page fixes can show movement within weeks. Broader category-level ranking takes longer and depends heavily on your specific niche's competition.
Is Google Ads worth it for a small online store?
Often yes, for specific high-intent product searches — the key is targeting narrow enough that you're not competing head-on with unlimited marketplace budgets.
Can an AI chatbot actually reduce cart abandonment?
It can answer the specific questions — sizing, stock, shipping — that cause hesitation at checkout. A meaningful reduction, not a complete fix on its own.
What should an online store budget for digital marketing?
Unlike most local businesses, an online store usually needs both SEO and paid ads running at the same time, not one or the other, so the real conversation is how to split spend between the two, not a single number.
